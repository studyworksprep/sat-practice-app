# Lesson bank revision review

> **Status: Historical.** Local revision and verification completed 2026-11-12. Production import remains pending.

Baseline: `64ed4698` on `main`, verified against the merged tutor-preview change. All 45 published bank lessons were inventoried on 2026-11-12. This revision covers the 43 lessons beyond the previously approved graphing and regression replacements. Those two specifications and their archives remain unchanged.

Each revised lesson has an ungraded opening attempt, a purpose and simpler starting example, a reusable procedure, relevant variations, a return to the opening question, recall before independent practice, and a short close. Explanation cards hide the calculator; action and answer-reading cards retain it when useful. Reused evidence stays in shared prompts.

The Subject–Verb Agreement lesson retains the existing he/they odd-one-out shortcut and all eligibility checks, as explicitly requested: four complete verb phrases, exclusive classification, and a 3-to-1 split. It does not replace that shortcut with subject-finding.

## Source preservation

The previous specifications for all 43 lessons are saved under `docs/lesson-template-specs/archive/`. The 38 originals already tracked in the repository preserve the baseline JSON content. Line endings and trailing blank lines were normalized in the archived Boundaries, Reading Comprehension, and Linear Inequalities files; the other 35 copies are byte-for-byte unchanged. Five published lessons absent from the repository were exported read-only and archived before revision: Graph Match, Central Ideas and Details, Cross-Text Connections, Text Structure and Purpose, and Multiple Percent Equations with List Regression. Their original block identifiers remain in the source and retained activities.

- Added missing correct-answer explanations and replaced confusing “assigned value” wording.
